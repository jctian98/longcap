LongCap: Hour-Scale Timestamped Audio Captioning via Chunk-wise Processing

LongCap describes the speech, music, and sound events in a recording with timestamps and structured attributes, and transcribes speech with speaker labels, from short clips to hours of audio.

About these labels and predictions

The reference retains the benchmark's known long events and annotation gaps. One mixed-speaker annotation at 4589–5010 seconds in the Oscars recording was excluded; no replacement labels were invented.

Predictions are the model's saved single pass over each full recording, kept with their errors. All event times and attributes are preserved. Three of the four outputs are marked partial because some chunks still failed after retries.

Browsing changes which events are visible; it does not modify the downloadable data. Original property names and annotation languages are preserved.
